Why I'm building Wickefi

I'm a dad, a full-time consultant and an aspiring entrepreneur.

Seven days a week, you'll usually find me somewhere around a soccer field, watching my kids chase their dreams.

But somewhere between working, paying bills, managing investments and keeping up with everyday responsibilities, I realized something.

I spend my professional life helping businesses operate more efficiently. Why wasn't I doing the same thing with my own life?

So I decided to build something different. An intelligent system that helps manage my finances, automate my responsibilities and build a business while allowing me to be present for my family.

Not because I want to work more.
Because I want to live more.

That's why I'm building Wickefi. I'm building it for myself first. And if it works, I'll show others how to do the same.

— Wesley, founder
